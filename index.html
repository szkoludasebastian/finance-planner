Features

Everything you need to manage money

No bloat, no subscriptions. Just the tools that matter.

Multiple Dashboards
Separate financial spaces for home, work, travel or any project — each with its own budget, categories and data.
Budget Tracking
Define spending limits per period and monitor progress in real time. A visual bar shows exactly how close you are to the edge.
Expense Management
Record one-time and recurring expenses with categories, comments and dates. Swipe to delete, tap to edit.
Income Tracker
Log recurring salaries and one-time payments. Your running balance is calculated automatically and plotted over time.
Visual Statistics
Pie charts and category breakdowns give you an instant read on where money goes. Filter by period, category or budget.
Scenario Simulation
Toggle simulation mode to exclude future expenses and instantly see how your balance and charts would look without them.
Excel Import
Bulk-import expenses from any .xlsx file. A visual column mapper lets you match your spreadsheet headers in seconds.
Export & Backup
Export any dashboard to a portable JSON file and restore it on any device. Your data is never locked in.
Wallet & Initial Balance
Set a starting wallet balance with a date. It anchors your balance timeline so every calculation starts from the right number.
How it works

Up and running in minutes

No sign-up, no setup. Open the app and go.

1
Create a dashboard
Choose between a Budget dashboard (expenses vs. limit) or a Finance Tracker (incomes + expenses). Name it anything you like.
2
Add your categories
Default categories are seeded automatically. Add your own to match how you actually spend money.
3
Log expenses or import from Excel
Tap + to add expenses manually, or upload an .xlsx file and map the columns in seconds.
4
See your stats
Head to the Stats tab for a full breakdown — charts, totals, balance over time, and category filters.
